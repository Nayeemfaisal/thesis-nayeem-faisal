# Contributing

This is Nayeem Faisal's bachelor's thesis research repository. Corrections and well-sourced dataset suggestions are welcome.

For each catalogue entry, provide a concise factual description, an authoritative dataset link, the associated publication, licence information when known, and the date on which the link was checked. Do not upload or redistribute third-party datasets unless their licence explicitly permits it.

Use British English consistently, preserve original dataset titles, and identify estimates or interpretations as such. A catalogue entry should not claim that a dataset is suitable for a thesis experiment until it has been assessed with `RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`.
