# Thesis Repository Guide

This repository preserves Nayeem Faisal's final bachelor's thesis and its public web companion.

## Source of truth

The final PDF at site/public/nayeem-faisal-thesis.pdf is the authoritative academic document. Website copy must remain consistent with its title, authorship, methods, findings, figures, tables, references, and limitations.

## Website structure

- site/app/page.tsx - portfolio entry point
- site/app/thesis/layout.tsx - thesis metadata
- site/app/thesis/page.tsx - interactive thesis companion
- site/public/nayeem-faisal-thesis.pdf - final paper
- site/public/thesis-pages/performance-comparison.jpg - thesis figure preview

## Maintenance principles

1. Preserve the final PDF without rewriting its academic content.
2. Use the paper itself as the source for website facts and quotations.
3. Do not present summaries as substitutes for the thesis.
4. Keep authorship clearly attributed to Nayeem Faisal.
5. Verify the production build after website changes.
